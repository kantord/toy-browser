# toy-browser

Pages, layout and scenes: navigate, hit-test, scroll, press and type, and turn a page into a `Scene`. Built on the engine and the vendored Blitz layout. `Images` chooses what a picture is drawn as (as it is, as its `alt` text, or not at all).

Part of [toy-browser](https://github.com/kantord/toy-browser), dual-licensed MIT or Apache-2.0.
